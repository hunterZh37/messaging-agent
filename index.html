Celeste

A personal mail client, for one person's own mailboxes.

Celeste brings one person's email, iMessage and WhatsApp together on their own computer, sorts what arrives, drafts replies they approve before sending, and answers questions about what is in there. It runs on that computer. There is no service to sign up for and no other users.

It signs in to Gmail through Google OAuth so it can read, organise and send that person's own mail.
