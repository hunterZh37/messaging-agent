Handover

  • Nothing goes until you press the button
  • The title and the date go over. The message body never does
  • If the Scheduling Agent refuses, the refusal is kept beside the thread, with a Retry

Privacy

Never leaves your Mac
  • Mail & chats
  • Attachments
  • Embeddings
  • Search index
  • Drafts
  • Your voice file
Only the model calls you make Sorting can be local and free
  • Blocked senders are never stored and never reach a model
  • No telemetry, no phone‑home, loopback only

Why

“Nearly ten inboxes, and the ones that mattered were never on top.”

  • I get a lot of messages from a lot of places: nearly ten Gmail accounts, WhatsApp, Messages, Slack
  • My clients are spread across all of them, so it was easy to miss something that mattered, or to mean to reply and never get back to it
  • I wanted one place that pulls the sources together, and to check all of them in one go like a todo list
  • One place turned out to be overwhelming on its own. The cut‑down that stuck was projects: I describe a project in a sentence, and messages get filed under the one they match
  • I was already pasting drafts into Claude to catch my grammar before sending, so it seemed worth putting that where the mail already was
  • Slack is in that list because it is in my life, not because Celeste reads it. Today: mail over IMAP and Outlook, plus iMessage and WhatsApp
