# Starter projects

What "Add starter projects" writes into an empty inbox, mirrored in
`packages/core/src/projects/projects.ts`. They are examples and nothing more:
edit them, delete them, replace them entirely.

The **description** is the part that matters. It is what gets embedded and
compared against each message, so it should describe the mail you actually
receive, in your own words. Names of people and companies are deliberately
left out: a project is a kind of mail, not a list of senders.

## Clients

Live client work. Statements of work, deliverables, invoices, scheduling with
people at client companies.

## Money

Bills, invoices, receipts, bank and card notices, tax correspondence, anything
about money owed or owed to me.

## Legal and admin

Contracts, insurance, licences, government and institutional notices, anything
with a deadline attached to a form.

## Recruiting

Candidates, introductions, interview scheduling, offers, and the threads that
follow them.

## Suppliers and tools

Vendors, SaaS subscriptions, renewals, support tickets, outage notices.

## Personal

Friends and family, travel, appointments, anything that is not work.

## Writing your own

- One line on what belongs, one on what does not, if the boundary is unclear.
- Concrete beats abstract: "renewal notices and outage emails from tools I pay
  for" files better than "vendors".
- Six to a dozen is a good number. Too many and they compete for the same mail.
